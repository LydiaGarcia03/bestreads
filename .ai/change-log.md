  - `catalogRanking.ts`: tiers are now word based: `wholeWords` (every typed word is a whole word of the title or authors, so "Children of Dune" matches "dune") over `prefix` (words only start with the text). Score = 10 for any match + tier × 3.5 + title closeness × 1 + log10(1 + readers) × 1.5: any match beats any non-match; whole words beat prefixes unless far more read; within a tier popularity decides. `titleCloseness` (exact core title 1, share of a longer title, 0.6 for the part before a subtitle, 0.5 for author searches) and `coreTitle` (bracketed edition notes removed); `workKey` uses the core title, so "Dune (Movie Tie-In)" merges into "Dune" and adds its readers, while "Dune: House Corrino" stays separate.
  - `openLibraryQuery`: `(exact words) OR (words with the last as prefix)`, so exact titles rank first at Open Library ("circe*" alone missed "Circe").
